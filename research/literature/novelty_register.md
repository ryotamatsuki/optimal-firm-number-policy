Canonical-form threats: binary participation games with common additive incentive, strategic substitutes, stable sets, facility-location/submodular coverage, second-best constrained optimization. P1 directly follows from elementary participation inequalities (not novel). S01 perturbation selector likewise elementary. S03 surviving Stage 6 claim: count target can differ from constrained planner count because best feasible composition at that count is inferior to another count. Absorption by a general second-best theorem still a serious risk; no universal novelty certification. Stage 7 maximin and future structural theorem require new source-specific absorption review.
