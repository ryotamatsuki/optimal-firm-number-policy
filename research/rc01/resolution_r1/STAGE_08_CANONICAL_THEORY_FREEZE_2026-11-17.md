## 2. Research question and contribution
Frozen working title: Policy Resolution and the Identification of Product Differentiation: A Circular-Market Model.
When can anonymous participation-policy experiments identify latent product differentiation, and when does a finite policy menu prevent learning and produce informational welfare loss relative to the same available policies with known geometry? Main P4: endogenous response creates an identification window whose endpoints are computable from full shares, not monotonically informative policy intensity. Outside it, the full compatible fiber can share observations; inside, under stated three-gap condition, every equilibrium of the qualifying state separates alternatives. P5: even near but not exactly equal initial shares, coarse feasible menus can force observationally equivalent states with disjoint robust optimal policies. Policy is a market-changing AND information-generating device. P2 continuum result benchmark; E/P1 background; P3 scoped continuum welfare antecedent. No new generic equilibrium, partial-ID, finite-instrument or minimax technique claimed.

## 5. Proposition register (unchanged exact scope)
All E, P1, P2, P3, P4a, P4b, P5 below are analytically PROVED in stated domains, not universally across markets. All have selected PROOF-CRITICAL CORE formal coverage under interface assumptions, not FULL CLAIM mechanization. E/P1 ancillary, P2/P3 benchmark, P4a/b primary result, P5 welfare consequence.
P1. For all admissible odd N, initial theta=0 identifies relative ordered gaps uniquely. For even N all compatible distinct positive solutions differ in one alternating direction d'_i-d_i=(-1)^i delta after indexing convention, delta nonzero with positivity. Adjacent sums unchanged, sum=1 unchanged. Even fiber one-dimensional, positivity strictly interior.
P2 (continuum benchmark). For every even N>=4 and distinct compatible d,d', [for every real theta O_d(theta) intersects O_d'(theta)] iff full q uniform. On any nonuniform full q, the protocol theta_k=H+2^(-k), k>=1, stopping when a retained share has nonzero coefficient on the alternating direction, identifies EVERY actual admissible d in finitely many queries for EVERY pure selector. Pointwise finite, not uniform count; initialization at zero. Suffices to choose theta between H and min(T,K), with definitions below. For uniform q, exists one common deterministic selector over the entire compatible family, with identical observable history for any adaptive query rule under coupled random seed. Not all selectors, not identical entire correspondences. 
P4a. For all even N>=4, define H=min_i h_i, T=min(max_even h_i, max_odd h_i), K(d)=min_i(d_i+d_(i+1)+d_(i+2)). Parity sums each 1; H<=2/N<=T<1, H=T iff full q uniform. For EVERY compatible positive gap fiber and EVERY theta<=H OR theta>=T there EXISTS one labeled common equilibrium signal across ENTIRE fiber. Below/inclusive H choose full. Above/inclusive T omit parity with max h=T, retain opposite parity; no-entry holds there. Restrict to its subsets, use min-cardinality no-entry set or deterministic strict-loss deletion, preserving no-entry and invariance. Even-arc sums, entrant and incumbent shares invariant; corners handled by E. Thus if A misses (H,T), there is a common admissible observational path across the fiber, including adaptive randomized experimenter with coupled seed, hence no uniformly guaranteed identification if at least two compatible states. Does not prohibit informative equilibria outside the interval.
P4b. For every distinct compatible pair with K(d)>=T OR K(d')>=T, and every H<theta<T, their observable-signal sets are disjoint. In a qualifying state all relevant pure NE omit >=1 site; theta<1 excludes <=1 active; two consecutive omissions imply a gap >=K>theta and profitable entry, impossible. Omitting one entire parity fails inactivity because some h>=T. Thus both one- and two-original-gap retained arcs occur; at a junction, the one-step arc differs by +/-delta and two-step is invariant, so a surviving share differs. Even when active sets coincide, observed quantities distinguish. Stronger useful proof core holds locally when H<theta<min(T,K(d)); H<K(d) by positive gaps, and nonuniform gives H<T (hence P2 dyadic termination). With actual qualifying geometry, initialization and one interior query suffice; in a class of qualifying geometries admitting distinct compatible states, menu intersects (H,T) iff guaranteed identification. Without K condition, possible interior overlap; K not claimed necessary, is generally latent. H,T observable, not unconditional applicability solely from observed shares. For regular mesh spanning window, spacing strictly less than width guarantees interior point, not converse; endpoints/offset matter. No universal bounded-query guarantee.
P3 (continuum welfare antecedent). Even N>=4; U gaps 1/N; C alternating a,b>0, a+b=2/N, common full shares. Assume ab/2<F/t<1/[4N(N-1)]. gamma=t/[4N(N-1)]-F>0, Delta=F-tab/2>0. U all-active unique fixed-site optimum implemented at 0; actions theta>H impose worst-pure excess cost>=gamma. In C choose H<theta<min(H+min(a,b),1); all NE have nonempty isolated omissions, save at least Delta versus full. theta<=H admits full and therefore worst-pure regret>=Delta. Distinct known-state robust-optimal policy sets, common path at all theta. Deterministic and randomized worst robust regret lower bounds min(gamma,Delta), gamma*Delta/(gamma+Delta), with benchmark below.
P5 (finite-menu welfare). For any even N>=4, a,b>0 with a<b and a+b=h0=2/N. Worlds dU_i=1/N+u_i, dC_i=(alternating a,b)_i+u_i, sum u=0 and max |u_i|<eta, same initial shares. A finite containing 0, h0 not in A, both nearest menu points L=max{x in A:x<h0}, R=min{x in A:x>h0} exist. Sufficient conditions 0<eta<min(a,1/N); 2eta<min(h0-L,R-h0); R<h0+a-3eta; t(a+eta)(b+eta)/2<F<t(1/N-eta)^2/2. Define gamma=t(1/N-eta)^2/2-F>0 and Delta=F-t(a+eta)(b+eta)/2>0. H>=h0-2eta>L; T<=h0+2eta<R, so all A miss open window. For U, iteratively adding missing sites to nonempty subset saves >=gamma each (split gain t ab/2 minus F for the split lengths, both >=1/N-eta); empty compared separately using v-c>t/2; full unique fixed-site optimum at 0. All theta>=R exclude full and robust loss>=gamma. For C at R, all pure NE omit at least one site, none consecutive (three-gap bound), save >=Delta each isolated omission (product at most (a+eta)(b+eta), additivity). Thus known-state R_C^*(A)<=C_C(full)-Delta; for each theta<=L full feasible, robust loss>=Delta. Same A partitions into low/high, distinct optima, identical possible history. Deterministic max regret>=min(gamma,Delta); randomized max expected robust regret>=gamma*Delta/(gamma+Delta). Nonuniform shares obtained on relatively open range of compatible-share space (sum-zero u excluding alternating kernel; not open independently-chosen pairs). No universal loss of every blind menu, no exact general minimax claim, no strict-boundary extension.
