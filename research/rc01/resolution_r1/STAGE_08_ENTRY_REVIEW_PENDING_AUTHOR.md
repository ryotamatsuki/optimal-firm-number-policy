## Candidate reading guide (AI explanation, not author's own answers)
Question: when can anonymous finite-resolution participation policy experiments identify latent product differentiation, and when do information constraints change robust welfare-maximizing decisions? N>=3 ordered distinct positive circle gaps summing to 1, independent of firm actions; uniform unit consumers; nearest active product at common fixed price, m=p-c>0, real fixed cost F>0, linear mismatch t>0, full coverage v-p>t/2; binary activity, pure Nash with weak inequalities; firms know gaps, regulator knows order but not gaps, exact labeled activity and quantities; fresh static experiments; policy menu A finite contains 0, same menu for experiments and terminal decision; no persistent intertemporal incentive. 
E/P1 background: doubled demand h_i=d_(i-1)+d_i at full activity. For S nonempty, q_i(S)=(adjacent retained gap sum)/2. Active stays iff 2q_i(S)>=theta; inactive no profitable entry iff 2q_i(S+i)<=theta, theta=2(F-s)/m. Odd cycle determines gaps; even compatible states can differ in an alternating direction. 
P2 continuum benchmark: nonuniform even shares can be identified in finitely many pointwise queries against every pure selection; uniform shares admit a common selector. No uniform upper count bound. 
P4a/P4b main: H=min h_i, T=min(max even h_i,max odd h_i), K(d)=minimum sum of three consecutive gaps. Under arbitrary pure NE selection, outside open (H,T) at least one common observable equilibrium signal exists across compatible even states, not all signals equal. For interior policy, K(d)>=T in at least one of the compared states implies disjoint signals; proof uses isolated omissions, exclusion of full parity omission, and an active junction with one one-step and one two-step retained gap. If actual state satisfies K>=T, initial and one interior experiment suffice. K is latent and not generally implied by h. Other geometries can have a common interior signal; do not claim a universal iff without domain condition. If A misses window, a shared selection path prevents guaranteed identification. 
P5 main welfare corollary: h0=2/N, a+b=h0, 0<a<b, paired gaps dU=1/N+u_i and dC=alternating a,b + same u_i, sum u=0, max |u_i|<eta. 0 in A, h0 not in A, adjacent L<h0<R; eta<min(a,1/N), eta>0, 2eta<min(h0-L,R-h0), R<h0+a-3eta, t(a+eta)(b+eta)/2<F<t(1/N-eta)^2/2. Benchmark R_d^*(A)=min over theta in A max over all pure equilibria cost C_d(S) with C=kF+t sum g^2/4 (empty v-c), not unrestricted first best. Known-state optimum differs across two worlds; worst terminal pure selection can depend on realized policy. gamma=t(1/N-eta)^2/2-F, Delta=F-t(a+eta)(b+eta)/2; lower bounds min(gamma,Delta) deterministic and gamma*Delta/(gamma+Delta) randomized.
