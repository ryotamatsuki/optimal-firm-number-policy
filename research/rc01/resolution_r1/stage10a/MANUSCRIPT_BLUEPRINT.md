## A. Narrative and reader arrival
Question: Can a regulator learn relative product distances by varying a common participation transfer and observing labeled operating firms and shares, when equilibrium selection is unknown? Stakes: a policy can remove exactly the variation that would distinguish markets, so correct intervention depends on learning as well as availability. Main economic mechanism: alternating changes in original gaps leave adjacent sums invariant; parity-based survival can preserve that invariance; an odd/even arc junction can reveal the missing component. Main result: an observable open threshold window admits all-selector separation under an explicit sufficient gap condition; outside it family-wide common signals EXIST. Continuum benchmark and finite-menu same-instrument welfare conflict follow.

## E. Cold-reader design checks (plan-level, AI self-critique, not Stage 11 independently reviewed)
1. Can reader say what is hidden? Relative labeled gap vector, not scalar competition intensity or complete preferences.
2. Why same quantities? Alternating perturbation exactly cancels adjacent/even arcs.
3. Why middle action? isolated exits plus not whole parity force a junction; requires latent K, not observable sufficient geometry merely because H,T measured.
4. Does failure mean impossible under every selection? No, common signal exists under admissible selection. Can high intervention sometimes reveal? Yes.
5. What is welfare counterfactual? Same available menu and worst pure criterion with known geometry, not first best or fixed persistent realized outcomes.
6. Are results universal? No; fixed prices, known order, uniform consumers, exact shares, signed transfer, relevant pure equilibria. Include K counterexample and blind menu with no welfare conflict.
Strongest predicted writing failure: a vivid opening diagram could make the middle condition appear unconditional or turn common possibility into inevitable ignorance. Require condition and signal quantifier in caption/near text; at Stage 11 cold-read actual draft before seeing blueprint. Other potential objection: a sprawling related literature section could delay the mechanism; integrate without losing source-level novelty guards. Mathematical proof and author/external sign-off remain distinct.
