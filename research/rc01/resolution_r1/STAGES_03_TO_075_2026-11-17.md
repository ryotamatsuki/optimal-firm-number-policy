Define h_i=d_(i-1)+d_i=2q_i(full), H=min h_i, T=min(max over even i h_i, max over odd i h_i), K(d)=min_i(d_i+d_(i+1)+d_(i+2)), oriented mod N. For even N>=4 each parity sum h_i=1; H<=2/N<=T<1. P4a (blind outside window, universal in even fiber): For every theta<=H all active is an admissible equilibrium, with identical full shares. For every theta>=T choose parity of idle sites whose maximum h_i equals T (fix label rule for ties); retain all sites of opposite parity. For each missing site, its containing retained gap = h_i; no-entry holds including equality. Among subsets of that retained parity choose a minimum-cardinality no-entry set; existence from finite set including retained parity. If any active firm strictly loses, dropping it preserves no-entry (merges two adjacent gaps to length 2q_i<theta; two->one merges to 1, one->zero requires theta>2). Contradiction. Thus chosen set is pure NE. All possible retained gaps within that parity, all entry-constraint lengths and observed quantities depend only on full h, not alternating delta. This gives one deterministic common selector for ENTIRE fiber at every theta outside (H,T). Includes boundary H,T and empty/singleton. If A misses (H,T), no adaptive protocol can guarantee structural identification: couple random seeds/induct over equal histories. This does not mean all NE signals coincide.
P4b (separation inside window under explicit geometry): if one of two distinct compatible d,d' has K(d)>=T, for every theta in (H,T), ALL pure NE of that geometry reveal delta. There must be some omissions, >=2 survivors (theta<1). Two successive omitted labels would entail containing gap of length >=K>=T>theta and a profitable entrant. Hence omissions isolated. If all retained cyclic gaps consist of two original gaps, exits exactly one parity, whose max h_i>=T, contradicting no-entry. So at least one one-step and at least one two-step retained gap exist; at a junction the doubled share changes by +/-delta, as one-step gap varies and two-step gap invariant. Therefore O_d(theta) and O_d'(theta) disjoint, and observing any such NE rules out every alternative on initial fiber even if alternative has that profile. If actual d meets K>=T, one revealing policy plus initialization suffices. For pair with at least one such geometry, intersection for all menu policies iff A misses (H,T). For a restricted class all d with K>=T, exact menu criterion for uniform-in-selection identification (provided comparison to distinct compatible worlds; outside window common selector also valid among this class). If K<T not necessary; lower near-H continuum theorem still holds; interior may or may not reveal. Odd-N initial shares identify as before. Uniform-share H=T gives empty window. Corollary for a regular mesh spanning window: spacing strictly less than T-H guarantees a mesh point inside, equality or larger spacing does not guarantee (offset matters). Not a necessity for particular offset or every geometry. No new universal bound on adaptive continuum query count.
