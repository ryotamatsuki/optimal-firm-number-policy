## 1. Primitives and complete equilibrium solution
Unit circle with distinct labeled sites in known oriented cyclic order, absolute rotation normalized; d_i>0 sum d_i=1, i modulo N. Uniform unit consumer mass, utility v-p-t*geodesic distance from nearest active product; outside option 0; v-p>t/2, t>0, m=p-c>0. Fixed prices and marginal cost, common real fixed cost F>0 per active firm, common transfer s in R (negative tax), no fiscal distortion. Complete information among firms. Regulator knows N, order, m,F,t etc., not gaps; observes exact labeled activity S and all realized shares q, not travel disutility or counterfactual demand. Each experimental round fresh simultaneous complete-information binary activity game; no intertemporal firm payoffs. Government can adapt theta=2(F-s)/m in R to exact past observations; unknown selection of pure Nash equilibrium, possibly history-dependent. No SPNE claim; selector treated as arbitrary admissible observation process. Pure NE only.

For S nonempty, consecutive active gaps g_j partition circle. Each arc split at midpoint (measure-zero ties); active share Q_j=(g_(j-1)+g_j)/2 and T(S)=t sum g_j^2/4 by integrating distance on both half arcs. For singleton g=1,q=1; for empty no sales, no utility. Firm payoff active mQ_i-F+s, inactive 0. For any subset S, full pure BR conditions are 2Q_i(S)>=theta for i in S, 2Q_i(S+i)<=theta for i outside S. For >=2 active, entry demand 2Q_i(S+i)=length of active gap containing i; same formula with singleton gap 1. Thus interval [L(S),H(S)] with L=max gap containing inactive firm (minus infinity when none), H=min adjacent active gap sum; empty [2,infinity). Every weak inequality included; interval with L>H means no policy implements S. This characterizes *all* pure NE at any parameter, not a closed-form selection of a unique equilibrium. FOCs/SOCs/Hessians/KKT/inverse demand N/A discrete activity and fixed exogenous price; all deviations evaluated. Nonempty for theta<2; all active unique for theta<=0; possibly ties and multiple equilibria. 
Existence lemma: start with all sites active and repeatedly delete a firm with 2q<theta. For >=3 active the two gaps adjacent to the deleted firm merge into a gap of length 2q<theta; every inactive firm in that merged gap has doubled entry-share equal to its length, so no profitable entry. All other inactive constraints unchanged. At 2->1 merged gap length 1<theta (both shares .5); at 1->0 requires theta>2, empty stable. Termination after <=N deletions; retained firms satisfied; no-entry invariant holds. Also valid starting with any S already satisfying no-entry inequalities. Handles exact boundary (delete only strict loss). E_d(theta) nonempty for every theta and history, multiple equilibria allowed. No solver-based SPNE or dynamic extension.

## 2. Preliminary certificates — P1 / P2
P1 (ancillary algebra): initial query theta=0 forces all active. Q_full satisfies 2q_i=d_(i-1)+d_i. Any two solutions differ by alternating delta*(-1)^i. Odd N forces zero difference; even N allowed interval of delta from positivity, automatically preserves sum. Thus point ID of relative ordered gaps for odd N; even N has 1-dimensional open feasible fiber. Not independent novelty. 
P2 (headline, global within fixed primitives): Let N even >=4 and d,d' distinct with identical full labeled share vector. Define O_d(theta) as signals (S,q_d(S)) over E_d(theta). Then O_d(theta) intersect O_d'(theta) nonempty for every real theta iff q_full uniform. If full q differs, query 0 separates. For nonuniform q, one adaptive protocol identifies actual d in finite time for every true positive geometry and every pure selector. Claims modulo rotation, orientation/order known; exact observations, not noisy; no full-correspondence equality or all-selectors impossibility.
Necessity and constructive identification: H=min_i h_i, h_i=d_(i-1)+d_i=2q_i. With nonuniform q, H<2/N; choose 0<epsilon<min{min d, min_{h_i>H}(h_i-H),1-H}. At theta=H+epsilon, all-active violates at least one stay condition and empty/singleton impossible (theta<1; entry of second firm has doubled share 1). Any omitted site i belongs to an active gap at least h_i, hence h_i=H. If two successive original sites are omitted, gap containing them >=H+min d>theta, contradiction. Thus omissions nonempty, isolated and all minimizers. Retained cyclic gaps are sums of one or two original gaps; under shift delta*(-1)^i their differences are +/-delta for length-one, 0 for length-two. If observed retained shares identical, all consecutive retained-gap differences sum to zero; because at least one gap difference is zero (some omission), all must be zero. Distinct delta therefore forces all gaps to have length two, so all members of one parity class omitted. Sum of full h_i over either parity equals 1. If all those h_i equal H, (N/2)H=1, contradict H<2/N. Hence any observed signal in true world's E_d(theta) at this theta rules out EVERY other compatible geometry, irrespective of whether those alternatives have a NE in same S. This is stronger than pairwise tests. For arbitrary nonuniform even q, regulator does not know min d. Compute any particular compatible d0 and null vector e; probe theta_k=H+2^(-k) for k>=1, each time evaluate known linear formula for retained shares L_S(d0+delta e) using indices. Stop when L_S e !=0; infer uniquely delta from any informative coordinate, check positivity. Bound epsilon>0 for each true d, so all sufficiently large k give a revealing S; pointwise finite under arbitrary selection, no uniform finite bound established. With unique minimum, one additional query at midpoint between H and second-smallest h_i suffices: any omitted site must be unique minimizer since entry gap>=h_i (singleton/empty excluded as theta<1); all-active impossible; sole omitted site, retained shares reveal. No need to know d for that choice. Initial plus one additional query, not one total. Equal-minimum ties treated by adaptive algorithm.
Sufficiency: uniform full shares implies alternating positive gaps a,b with a+b=2/N. For theta<=H=2/N, all active is common equilibrium (not always unique); for theta>H, start from retained even-label parity. Active gaps initially 2/N, every omitted site in such gap, so no-entry satisfied. Delete strict-loss active firms in fixed label priority until pure equilibrium using existence lemma. Since surviving labels are within one parity whose pairwise arc differences and all gaps depend solely on N and retained indices, all deletion decisions and resulting labeled quantities invariant across alternating a,b (including singleton/empty). Thus one common signal at each theta and common fixed deterministic selector. Couple experimenter's random seed and induct over adaptive history; identical histories and stopping/terminal rule distributions, hence no protocol uniformly identifies actual geometry across this class. This is not saying no revealing selection exists. Prior Stage 1 N=4 theta=.6 correspondences differ, theta=.5 triples may reveal; retain counterexamples. If N=3 works odd rank; coincident endpoints a=0 or b=0 excluded; unknown ordering and mixed NE excluded. Symmetry exception is lower-dimensional (codimension N-2 among even N positive gaps); positive identification off that locus is central. No empirical universality or noise claim.

## 3. P3 welfare consequence (precise benchmark and proof)
For nonempty S, CS=v-p-T; profit aggregate m-|S|F+|S|s; fiscal balance -|S|s; welfare W=v-c-|S|F-T. Empty W=0. Resource cost C_d(S)=|S|F+T, C(empty)=v-c. Same consumer mass, valuations, price, costs across worlds. Fixed-site planner min_S C, not unrestricted first best. For information loss define policy loss criterion R_d(theta)=max_{S in E_d(theta)} C_d(S); known-state restricted-instrument robust optimum R_d^*=min_theta R_d(theta). Finite outcome intervals imply min attained (enumerate finite endpoints and open intervals), all NE exist. This benchmark does not assume planner implementable in general. Policy comparisons use worst pure outcomes, not mixed NE.
For even N>=4, U equal gaps 1/N, C alternating a,b with a+b=2/N, a,b>0; common shares 1/N. Suppose a*b/2 < F/t < 1/[4N(N-1)] (nonempty e.g. sufficiently small a). Let gamma=t/[4N(N-1)]-F>0 and Delta=F-t*a*b/2>0. In U, for any k in 1..N-1, Jensen/Cauchy T>=t/(4k). Cost excess relative full >=(N-k)(t/(4kN)-F)>=gamma. Empty also strictly worse by >=gamma: C_full+gamma=(N-1)F+t/(4(N-1))<(t/(4N)+t/(4(N-1)))<t/2<v-c. Full unique planner optimum, attained as unique pure NE at theta=0; thus R_U^*=C_U(all). For theta>H full not NE, so R_U(theta)-R_U^*>=gamma.
In C pick H<theta<H+min(a,b) and theta<1. Every pure NE omits >=1 firms; omissions isolated by entry constraint (if >=2 omissions in a gap then length >=H+min(a,b)>theta). Empty/singleton impossible. Each isolated omission replaces two original neighboring gaps (a,b) by a+b, increases T by t*a*b/2 and saves F; increments additive for isolated omissions (including two active antipodal). With r omissions, C_C(all)-C_C(S)=r*Delta>=Delta. Thus R_C^*<=C_C(all)-Delta. At theta<=H full is NE, so R_C(theta)-R_C^*>=Delta. The two sets of known-state robust optimal policies are disjoint, even though all experimental thresholds admit common observations. Positive welfare gap not caused by transfer accounting. No claim every paired planner optimum implementable; Stage1 N=4 stronger special case retained separately.
For a deterministic terminal action chosen on common history, known-state robust policy regret max over U,C >=min(gamma,Delta). If regulator randomizes terminal theta, evaluate each realized action by R_d(theta) (adversarial terminal pure selection conditional on action, allowing distinct experiment/implementation selection). Under common experimental selector, distribution same. Let p=P(theta<=H); nonnegative robust regrets imply max expected regret >=max[(1-p)gamma,p Delta] >=gamma Delta/(gamma+Delta). This is a lower bound, not general exact minimax value; follows familiar binary decision theory (Manski), not novelty. If a *fixed* selector must persist through terminal execution, this random robust regret interpretation is not automatically a bound on realized cost minus robust optimum; do not assert it. Separate guaranteed conflict of robust policy objectives and planner-relative loss. The bound applies to the stated robust criterion; actual dynamic selection experiments and costs not modeled. Pure equilibrium not mixed-inclusive; Stage1 example already falsifies such extension.
