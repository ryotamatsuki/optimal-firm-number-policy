## Preferred minimal mechanism and precise proposed propositions
Maintain unit circle, uniform unit mass, fixed common price/margin m>0, t>0, real F>0, full coverage v-p>t/2, distinct positive ordered gaps d_i summing to 1, known labeled cyclic order, unknown relative geometry, binary simultaneous activity, real common transfer, threshold theta=2(F-s)/m. Observations exact active labels and quantities; no outside data on disutility. Fresh static experiments, unknown pure NE selector (possibly history-dependent; no dynamic profit). Full active unique for theta<=0. N>=3 arbitrary, only enlargement is number of existing players. Compare same cyclic order modulo rotation; no claim of absolute location/unknown ordering. S=empty, singleton as Stage1.
H=min_i(d_(i-1)+d_i)=2min q_full. Observational equivalence of two distinct worlds: for every real theta there exists a common signal (same S and labeled q) in both pure NE correspondences. This implies admissible deterministic common selector and impossibility of uniformly identifying from adaptive histories; not equality of correspondence or all selectors. Pairwise separation alone does not prove a finite data-dependent identifying protocol; test that separately.
P1 (ancillary known algebra): odd N full-active q determines d; even N compatible gaps d'=d+delta*(-1)^i with positivity and same total. Not headline novelty.
P2 (main candidate, exact boundary): for even N>=4, two distinct gap vectors with identical full-active labeled q are observationally equivalent over all common thresholds iff q_i=1/N for all i. If full shares differ, initial active observation already separates. If N odd and known order, point ID from initial observation. At nonuniform even q, a finite adaptive sequence of pure-equilibrium observations should identify the actual d for every admissible pure selector at true positive geometry. With unique minimum full share, one subsequent threshold strictly between lowest and second-lowest full adjacent sum should force unique first exit; then inversion. Tied minimum: query theta_k=H+2^-k approaching H from above; terminate when observed subset has nonzero coefficient on delta in reduced share equation. No uniform finite bound claimed over all positive d.
Proof strategy for Stage4, not certification: at theta just above H, compare inactive entry gap to original adjacent sums; if an omitted firm is not a minimizer, or two consecutive omitted sites, entry gap exceeds theta if epsilon<min positive gap and min nonminimum adjacent sum difference. Exclude empty/singleton near H (H<=2/N, choose theta<1). So omitted sites isolated and all are minimizers. Retained reduced gaps consist of one or two original gaps. Under alternating perturbation, a one-step gap changes by +/-delta, a two-step gap is invariant. If reduced labeled shares unchanged, differences of retained gaps alternate, so cannot mix zero/nonzero. Since proper subset (all-active fails), all steps must be two, thus half the firms of a parity exit. Original adjacent sums over any parity add to 1, so if all these sums equal H then H=2/N and all original sums equal (contradicts nonuniform). Therefore any observed NE in sufficiently small first-exit interval reveals delta. Pure existence for all theta to be proved using deletion from all active of any firm with 2q<theta, merged gap length <theta, preserve inactive no-entry; explicitly handle 2->1,1->0, thresholds equality. In equal-share case gaps alternate a,b with a+b=2/N. For theta<=2/N keep all. For theta>2/N retain one parity (equidistant and invariant up to rotation) then delete losing firms iteratively; inactive gap constraints preserved, subset signals and deletion choice independent of a, empty if theta>2. This yields common selector for all equal-share geometries. Test all boundaries and prove the actual algorithm; cannot assume complete-equilibrium equality. Finite adaptive trial termination pointwise, not uniform or noisy. 
P3 (secondary welfare target): compare evenly spaced d=1/N and paired d=(a,b) repeated, a+b=2/N, a small positive. F/t in (a*b/2,1/[4N(N-1)]) and v-c large enough for empty not optimal (coverage and upper bound suffice; verify). Uniform world full-active unique planner minimizer (Cauchy lower bound for n<N). In paired world pick theta just above H=2/N and within a (minimum gap), all omissions isolated, no full-active NE, each omission reduces social resource cost by F-t*a*b/2>0, including multiple isolated omissions (travel increments additive). Thus all pure NE at that threshold improve on full-active. Both states share policy signals along common selector, but uniform optimum requires keeping all (threshold <=H), paired robust-optimal policy cannot be such if full active is possible and a robust improvement exists. Need prove exact regret lower bound or distinguish benchmark known-state worst-pure vs planner; do NOT assert paired global planner feasible for every N. Recover four-site Stage1 stronger special case. This is attached welfare consequence not new mechanism, no generic novelty for minimax formula. Neutral transfers and same v,c,t,F across worlds essential.
Falsifiers: distinct positive equal-share states lacking common signal at some threshold; nonuniform equal-full-q pair covering all common thresholds; near-H nonrevealing NE; deletion algorithm fails existence/corners; odd inverse fails with order known; geometric result already direct theorem; no meaningful welfare action distinction. Stop/reject if only parity algebra or four-site numerical example survives.
