## Frozen result and canonical skeleton
State z determines payoff tables and real social costs; agents i choose a_i in {0,1}; active payoff normalized g_i(z,a_-i)-h where h=(F-s)/m; payoff for inactive 0. g_i is nearest-product market share, weakly decreasing when others enter. Regulator varies common h, observes full labeled activity and sales, not geometry or real mismatch costs. Pure NE E_z(h); observed signals O_z(h)={ (a,q_z(a)): a in E_z(h)}. A fixed common selection path exists if compatible signals for the two particular states at each h; same histories by induction under adaptive experiments. State-wise planner optimum achievable by known-state uniform scalar action (robust over pure NE in the example). Generic partial identification implies that if same histories admit incompatible optimal actions, no uniformly optimal decision rule; regret formulas are pre-existing. Thus parent theory takes signal/feasible outcome sets as given; the residual economic task is to derive how the shape of the demand substitution geometry and incentive inequalities determine intersections of O_z(h), and whether the observations are sufficient for welfare decision despite nonidentification of primitives. Baseline's antipodal family is a constructive existence example, not a generic characterization. Weak strategic substitutes, no random shocks, prices fixed, no intertemporal incentives. Mixed NE permit revealing data; not all selection paths identical. No model modification here.

Generic intersection argument and two-state equalization ABSORBED. Remaining candidate is a NEW RESULT IN A KNOWN MODEL CLASS, specifically derive economic conditions under which endogenous participation fails to create welfare-relevant identifying variation even when each state individually permits efficient implementation; distinguish selection-induced invisibility from nonidentification that would remain even with forced observations. The candidate only provisionally survives: no proof of generalization or priority. A formal reversal of BH or Berry-Haile is NOT claimed; their estimands/information differ.
Kill: general partial ID -> decision difficulty; minimax formula; common-vs-individual intervention difference touted as novel; generic product-removal / rank lemma standalone; all-equilibria equivalence (false Stage1); broad claims about universal insufficiency or policy irrelevance.
