## 2. Benchmarks, private vs social decisions
B0 fixed-site constrained planner: minimize C_d(S), S any subset including empty, known d, technology, fixed prices and full coverage when nonempty, no entry incentive restriction. Not unrestricted first best.
B1 known-state restricted-instrument robust optimum R_d*=min over real theta of max_{S in E_d(theta)} C_d(S), theta=2(F-s)/m; worst pure NE, state d known. Finite interval regimes and existence ensure minimum attained. For each d, B1>=B0, sometimes strict.
B2 unknown-state same-instrument robust decision: terminal theta chosen by experimental history, with distinct U/C worlds and common observational selector witness. Evaluate loss R_d(theta)-R_d*, taking worst terminal pure equilibrium conditional on chosen action (need not equal experimental selector); max over possible d. For stochastic terminal theta compare expected robust loss and minimize across distributions; no realized persistent-selector welfare or mixed NE claim. Experiments costless; lower bound on regret under common histories, exact values below only for numerical two-world robust terminal problem. 
Private entry if 2q_i(S+i)>theta; private strict exit if 2q_i(S)<theta. Social trade-off of adding a site splitting an active gap into a,b: C(S+i)-C(S)=F-t ab/2 (nonempty base). Private entry demand (a+b)/2 depends on sum rather than product, revealing different cost relevance of placement. Profit equality permits either own action; not harmless for other payoffs; Stage4A regression retained. Empty entry compared to v-c vs F+t/4; no nonempty formula used for empty. 
P3 certified condition: even N>=4, U gaps 1/N and C alternating a,b>0, a+b=2/N; ab/2<F/t<1/[4N(N-1)]. Let gamma=t/[4N(N-1)]-F>0, Delta=F-tab/2>0, H=2/N. U fixed-site optimum uniquely all active, achieved by theta=0; for any theta>H every pure NE nonfull and excess cost>=gamma. In C, sufficiently small theta-H>0 implies all pure NE omit nonempty isolated firms, C falls by >=Delta. At theta<=H all-active is a possible equilibrium and hence worst-case robust regret>=Delta. Do not say all pure outcomes at low theta are inefficient. Thus incompatible robust policy objectives, despite common experimental history existence; known-state benchmark not assumed equal planner. Under common history, deterministic max robust regret>=min(gamma,Delta); randomized expected max robust regret>=gamma*Delta/(gamma+Delta). Generic formula already known; no new minimax theorem.
