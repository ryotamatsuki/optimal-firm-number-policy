## S01 archive (inactive)
Circle of circumference 1; incumbent positions A=0, B=.25, C=.5, D=.75; equal margin .5, transport coefficient 1, F_A=.110, F_B=F_C=F_D=.050, F_E=.107. Entrant chooses nonentry or committed entry and arbitrary location; incumbents then simultaneously choose survival; consumers choose nearest active firm, equal split at coincident locations. Density 1+epsilon*cos(2*pi*(x-.05)), 0<epsilon<=.01. B/C/D strictly stay; A stays without entry and exits at z=.10. All continuation choices at zero profit allowed. Entrant's coexistence profit negative; upon replacement its demand on (-.25,.25) is .25+epsilon*sin(pi/4)*cos(pi*z-.1*pi)/pi, maximized at .10. Outcome unique, off-path strategies not necessarily unique. Uniform-demand limit has multiple replacement locations and Delta W=.003-z^2/2 (both signs); do not claim uniform-limit welfare loss for every equilibrium. For positive epsilon as specified, Delta W<=-.0007 by bound. At epsilon=.01, Delta W approx -.002035. Stage 6 did not establish full prior-art identity; paper-investment NO-GO because the perturbation selector is elementary and independent economic contribution weak.
