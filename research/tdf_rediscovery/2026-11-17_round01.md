## STANDARD candidate ledger
### R-C01 (Route C, first priority): When Market Data Cannot Identify the Optimal Industry Structure
Contribution: policy-information and identification boundary, not another count paradox. B0 adds an information restriction: government observes all labeled firm sales/count/profits, F,m,t, circular order (for matrix result), but not location gaps, travel loss, substitution geometry; experiments are common transfers, each round a fresh static game, and admissible unknown equilibrium selection. Firms know relevant primitives. No state persistence or forced experiment in baseline.
Four sites U=(0,.25,.50,.75), C=(0,.05,.50,.55), F/t=.02, t=1, v-c=2. Full-active shares 1/4 each in both markets. Planner optimum U all four cost .1425, C two opposite firms cost .165, all four cost .1825. Exact 16 subsets per market. At theta<=.5 all active is a common NE; .5<theta<=1 pair AC; 1<theta<=2 singleton A; theta>2 empty. This exhibits a common pure-equilibrium selection function for every real theta (NOT equality of entire NE correspondence). Hence all adaptive common-transfer-only experiments can have identical labeled sales histories in the two worlds, yet goals differ. Distinct information regimes: if all possible equilibria observable, or location/access known, identification may return. Under this path deterministic minimax regret >=7/400; randomized terminal-policy bound >=63/6400 (given same terminal distribution, loss of 2 vs 4 in U=9/400, loss of 4 vs 2 in C=7/400; worse alternatives cannot improve). In C, .5<theta<.55 all pure NE are opposite pairs (same optimum); in U theta<.5 all active strictly dominant. No mixed-selection guarantee for C.
Mechanism: q_i=(d_(i-1)+d_i)/2; welfare travel cost t*sum d_i^2/4. If cyclic order known, matrix I+shift has rank n for odd n, n-1 for even n; even kernel alternating +/- gaps. One observed gap identifies remaining scalar; one targeted removal of B with other firms fixed at initial all-active configuration reveals changes to adjacent shares (U: A,C=3/8 each; C: A=19/40,C=11/40; D=1/4). Generally neighbor gains b/2 and a/2. Such a targeted experiment is not a uniform transfer, and feasibility must be modeled; it is a separate benchmark, not an authorized policy redesign. Without knowing order the inversion claim does not apply. Need establish robust significance beyond physical geography (latent horizontal differentiation), and whether experiment impossibility survives credible selection restrictions/noise and alternative microfoundation. Core insight must be more than generic observational equivalence or rank deficiency.
Closest: Nocke & Schutz (2018) sufficient firm types under particular CES/logit systems; Kleven (2021) scope of sufficient stats; Conlon & Mortimer (2021, NBER 2018 rev 2020) diversion ratios from different interventions; Athey & Wager (2021) policy learning presupposes causal identification. Abstract-level only in current round. Direct threat AUDITABLE: search exact policy-experiment observational equivalence/identification in spatial product-choice and partial-ID theory; if existing theorem gives same constructive obstruction + remedy, narrow or reject. EPOCH-style claim remains HOLD; no cross-domain proof.
Status PROMISING, mathematical exploratory counterexample + analytic outline, novelty UNRESOLVED. Preferred separate Stage 0 if selected (information question changes); do not overwrite S03.

### R-A01 (Route A): Can anonymous policy guarantee efficiency without location information?
Same B0, target all pure equilibria cost guarantee under a single uniform instrument using only F,t,m. Suppose 0<F/t<1/8, theta=sqrt(8F/t) in (0,1), s=F-m*theta/2. Pure equilibrium exists: start all sites active; delete an active site whenever adjacent-gap sum <theta. Merged gap <theta preserves no-profitable-entry condition for any inactive in it. Stops; when only two active, each share 1/2 so no deletion; N=1 trivial. For any pure NE S, sum q=1 and q_i>=theta/2 -> |S|<=2/theta. For inactive j, gap d<=theta and splitting into a,b reduces travel cost by t ab/2 <=t theta^2/8=F. Because marginal access gains diminish (pointwise nearest distance), comparing with constrained planner R yields T(S)-T(R)<=F|R\S|; C(S)-C(R)<=F|S\R|<=2F/theta. By Cauchy, C(R)>=sqrt(tF), so C(S)/C(R)<=1+1/sqrt(2). This is an analytic candidate bound on real resource COST, not a percentage welfare-loss guarantee and not claimed sharp. Nonempty optimum ensured by F/t<1/8, v-p>t/2. No mixed equilibrium guarantee; common net tax may be necessary. Exact enumeration sampled 960 geometries (N 2–9, 120 draws each, rational centesimal sites and theta .01–.99, seed 20261117); 5672 pure NE checked, max observed ratio 1.3153498562. No counterexample; not proof of sharpness. 
Closest Levi, Perakis & Romero (2019), accepted version pp25–27 theorem 3 and conclusion: worst-case uniform per-unit co-payment consumption guarantee under Cournot/price-taking and budget; explicitly requests broader competition models. Different choice, objective, policy and fixed cost; not same theorem on face of verified result. Fournier & Scarsini (2019) asymptotic PoA/stability for fixed number retailers choosing location on networks (abstract); Dobson & Karmarkar (1987) stable facility sets (abstract); valid utility games/submodular approx (abstract/excerpt). Direct threat AUDITABLE: check stable-set algorithms, approximation of facility opening games, sharpness, mixed and nonnegative-only constraints; stop if elementary known approximation corollary with no economic value. Strong route, author-stated predecessor limitation, but possible narrow continuation rather than independent discovery.
Status PROMISING, mathematical analytic proof sketch + tests, novelty UNRESOLVED. If selected, existing strategic primitives retained, re-screen actual stronger result with Stage 2/3 as needed then 4/4A/6.

### R-A02 (Route A): Structural sufficient conditions for count and maximin mismatch
Five-site family (0,a,2a,2a+b,2a+b+e), b=1/2-a; 0<a<1/6, 0<e<a, (a^2+e(b-e))/4<F/t<a^2/2 (nonempty interval required). Candidate: unique fixed-site planner ABCD, best pure implementable set ACD, dominance-solvable theta=b+min(e,a-e)/2, hence maximin over all NE optimum (given pure-existence at all thresholds, re-audit endpoints). A,B,C marginal removal losses at full set exceed F; D preferred to E by symmetry; E redundant; H(ABCD)=2a<G=b; ABCE likewise nonimplementable. ACD beats 4-site implementable and all 5 under inequalities; full analytic enumeration of all alternatives still required before certification. Exact enumeration 82 grid instances, F/t midpoint of interval; strict dominance verified. Original (.12,.38,.01,.005) in domain. Closest same as baseline; additional economic mechanism limited (generalized example); AUDITABLE.
