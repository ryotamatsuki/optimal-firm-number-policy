## Hard gates before submission
- Full-length original research article, not letter/short paper.
- No mandatory submission fee, publication fee, page/length charge or other required charge. Subscription route may avoid APC; do not equate APC-free with zero total cost. Historical checks did not establish explicit zero submission and all mandatory charges for JICT. Confirm with current official rules or editor.
- AI use compatible with actual research and writing workflow, including substantial intellectual assistance. Verify journal/publisher-specific conditions, disclose accurately, ensure genuine author responsibility and contribution. No individual editorial permission sought.
- Match both subject and actual research level; use comparable recent full papers rather than impact factor alone. No acceptance probability inferred.

Previous discussion: JICT first target; JPET, Journal of Economics, Studies in Microeconomics, Economics and Business Review, RIO considered; fee/AI fit varies and is not established for all. BEJTE deprioritized due to strict AI-generated paper policy concerns; Economics Letters is a short-paper route and no longer target.
